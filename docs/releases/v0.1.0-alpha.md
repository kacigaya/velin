First preview of Velin, a native Android mail client for Tuta. **Read only.** Do not use it as your main mail app.

## What works

- Sign in with your Tuta account, including two-step verification with an authenticator code.
- Folders and labels.
- The mail list, with paging.
- Opening mail and conversations. Mail bodies are decrypted on the device. Remote images stay blocked until you allow them.
- Attachments. Downloads stop at 20 MiB. Not yet tested with real mail.
- Light and dark themes, dynamic color, and phone and tablet layouts.

## Not yet available

- Writing, replying and sending mail.
- Moving, archiving and deleting mail.
- Search, notifications and offline mode.
- Sign-in with a security key.

## Known limits

- Velin cannot yet verify sender signatures. Velin never shows a mail as verified.
- The pinned Tuta SDK includes the Rust `rsa` crate 0.9.8, which has a timing side-channel advisory without a fixed version ([RUSTSEC-2023-0071](https://rustsec.org/advisories/RUSTSEC-2023-0071.html)). The official Tuta app uses the same version.
- The APK supports 64-bit ARM and x86 devices only.

## Verify the download

Compare the SHA-256 checksum of the APK with `SHA256SUMS`.
