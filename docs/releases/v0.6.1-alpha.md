Patch release of Velin, a native Android mail client for Tuta. It fixes layout, contrast and icon problems in v0.6.0-alpha. Do not use it as your main mail app yet.

## Fixed

- Read mail now shows in grouped cards, like unread mail. A selected read mail no longer has a broken half-rounded shape.
- Unread mail cards are darker and read mail cards are lighter, so unread mail stands out more.
- Your avatar in settings is visible again. It could have the same color as the account card.
- Settings no longer shows "Your account" twice. Remote images is now under Privacy.
- Contrast and Accessibility in settings use matching icons.
- On tablets, no gray strip shows between the mail list and the mail pane.
- Back, reply, forward, send and other arrow icons now point the correct way in right-to-left languages.

## Known limits

The known limits of [v0.6.0-alpha](https://github.com/kacigaya/velin/releases/tag/v0.6.0-alpha) still apply.

## Update from v0.6.0-alpha

Install the new APK over the old one. Both use the same signing key, so your saved account and saved mail stay.

## Verify the download

Compare the SHA-256 checksum of the APK with `SHA256SUMS`.
