Fifth preview of Velin, a native Android mail client for Tuta. Velin now checks sender keys, lets you check a recipient's key by hand, and reports spam and phishing to Tuta. Do not use it as your main mail app yet.

## New

- Velin checks the key of each sender who uses TutaCrypt. It compares the key with the key that Velin trusts for that address. If the keys do not match, the mail shows a warning. Velin trusts the first key that it sees for an address.
- Check a recipient's key by hand. In a new mail, tap a Tuta recipient to see the key fingerprint. Compare it with the fingerprint in the recipient's own app. Then mark the key as checked, or reject it. Velin does not send to a rejected recipient, also after a restart.
- Report spam. When you move mail to Spam, Velin asks if it should also report the mail to Tuta. Select **Report** or **Move only**. In the mail list, Velin sends the report only after the undo time ends.
- Report phishing from the menu of an open conversation. Velin marks the mail as phishing, moves it to Spam and reports it to Tuta. You cannot undo this.
- After you send a reply or a forward, Velin marks the original mail as replied or forwarded.

## Fixed

- A new mail did not show in the mail list until you refreshed the list.
- On some accounts, Velin could not open a mail that another Tuta app had marked as replied or forwarded.
- Move to trash from an open conversation sometimes did nothing.

## Not yet available

- Sending to addresses outside Tuta, with or without a password.
- Attachments in new mail, reply and forward, and scheduled sending.
- Search, notifications and contact suggestions.
- Sign-in with a security key.

## Known limits

- Velin shows no "verified" mark for a sender. Only mail sent with TutaCrypt gets the sender key check.
- Velin does not check mail for phishing on the device, as the official Tuta app does.
- Velin asks about a spam report each time. It does not keep your answer.
- A forward does not include the attachments of the original mail. Velin tells you how many files it left out.
- The quote in a reply or forward keeps the text and links of the original mail, not its formatting or images. The quote header is in English.
- Velin deletes opened attachments from its cache after one hour, at the next app start or when you sign out.
- Velin keeps 200 mails per folder and the last 100 mails you opened. A mail you never opened cannot open offline.
- Velin keeps its own list of trusted keys. Keys you checked in the official Tuta app do not carry over.
- The pinned Tuta SDK includes the Rust `rsa` crate 0.9.8, which has a timing side-channel advisory without a fixed version ([RUSTSEC-2023-0071](https://rustsec.org/advisories/RUSTSEC-2023-0071.html)). The official Tuta app uses the same version.
- The APK supports 64-bit ARM and x86 devices only.

## Update from v0.4.0-alpha

Install the new APK over the old one. Both use the same signing key, so your saved account and saved mail stay.

## Verify the download

Compare the SHA-256 checksum of the APK with `SHA256SUMS`.
