Fourth preview of Velin, a native Android mail client for Tuta. You can now reply to and forward mail, and open the files that you receive. Do not use it as your main mail app yet.

## New

- Reply, reply all and forward. Velin fills in the recipients, the sender address and the subject, and quotes the original mail. The sent reply goes into the same conversation.
- If you close a reply or forward without a change, Velin does not keep a draft.
- Open received attachments. Velin decrypts the file and opens it in another app on your device. If no app can open the file, Velin tells you.

## Fixed

- Received attachments did not show when the sender gave each file a content ID, which Gmail often does. Velin now hides only images that show in the mail text.
- Attachments could not be downloaded. Several errors caused this, including for mail that Velin restored from its offline copy.
- Nested quotes were lost when you edited a saved draft.
- In Trash and Spam, screen readers announced the delete button as "Move to trash". It deletes permanently, and now says so.

## Not yet available

- Sending to addresses outside Tuta, with or without a password.
- Attachments in new mail, reply and forward, and scheduled sending.
- Search, notifications and contact suggestions.
- Checking a recipient's key fingerprint by hand. If a key changed, the only fix now is to remove that recipient.
- Sign-in with a security key.

## Known limits

- A forward does not include the attachments of the original mail. Velin tells you how many files it left out.
- Velin does not mark the original mail as replied or forwarded.
- The quote in a reply or forward keeps the text and links of the original mail, not its formatting or images. The quote header is in English.
- Velin deletes opened attachments from its cache after one hour, at the next app start or when you sign out.
- Velin keeps 200 mails per folder and the last 100 mails you opened. A mail you never opened cannot open offline.
- Velin keeps its own list of trusted recipient keys. Keys you verified in the official Tuta app do not carry over.
- Velin cannot yet verify sender signatures on received mail. Velin never shows a mail as verified.
- The pinned Tuta SDK includes the Rust `rsa` crate 0.9.8, which has a timing side-channel advisory without a fixed version ([RUSTSEC-2023-0071](https://rustsec.org/advisories/RUSTSEC-2023-0071.html)). The official Tuta app uses the same version.
- The APK supports 64-bit ARM and x86 devices only.

## Update from v0.3.1-alpha

Install the new APK over the old one. Both use the same signing key, so your saved account and saved mail stay.

## Verify the download

Compare the SHA-256 checksum of the APK with `SHA256SUMS`.
